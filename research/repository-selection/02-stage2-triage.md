# Stage 2 — Shortlist and integration-feasibility screen

**Status:** desk triage only. These are candidates for safe inspection and later decision-changing experiments, not adoption recommendations. There is no final foundation choice and no code was run.

Every repository candidate is currently **viable to inspect only**. None is **viable to test** until a separate Stage 3 T0 screen is completed after the user reviews this checkpoint.

The **primary shortlist contains 11 repository/capability leads plus one custom-core comparator**. Synthea and Kitware Pulse are pinned as watchlist items because they do not serve the assumed ECG-only slice; Pulse becomes relevant if treatment response is approved, while Synthea becomes relevant if realistic longitudinal charts are required. This keeps the active shortlist within the plan's 8–15 range without treating future options as first-slice candidates.

## Decision frame

The first-slice assumption in [Stage 0](00-requirements.md) is a scripted fictional encounter, one 12-lead ECG, diagnosis, and action/evidence debrief. The two foundation candidates below are alternatives; do not combine two full case engines. The custom core is a deliberately honest baseline that measures whether adopting a large game saves actual effort.

## Net effort hypothesis against a simple substitute

The comparison below makes the expected development-effort tradeoff explicit. Every net-effect statement is a **D-grade desk inference**, not measured engineering time. “Could save” means only the named scope might be avoided; it does not include clinical review, rights clearance, accessibility work, or the trusted case authority unless stated. If the simple substitute is sufficient, the larger upstream capability is not automatically a saving.

| Candidate / capability | Smallest same-role substitute | Work it could replace | Likely net effect for the assumed slice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Open Sim Lab** | Small authored encounter UI and case loop | Scenario progression, action handling, replay/debrief UI, and common game screens. | **Unresolved; could be neutral or negative.** The potential saving is substantial only if its scenario contract and UI can be kept. Mapping open-ended history and ECG result release may cost more than the small custom loop. |
| **GTAgentClinic** | Small local encounter UI with scripted patient answers | Broad ED interaction, orders/results, charting, local simulation server, and scoring/debrief. | **Likely negative unless the target explicitly wants its 3D ED experience.** Retaining its end-to-end application could avoid many screens, but simplifying its runtime and product shape is unmeasured work. |
